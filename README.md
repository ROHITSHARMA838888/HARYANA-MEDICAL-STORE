## 📖 Overview
**Haryana Medical Store** is a comprehensive, open-source, and lifetime-free Point of Sale (POS) and Inventory Management application specifically designed for pharmacies and medical stores. It aims to simplify day-to-day pharmacy operations, from billing to stock management, ensuring seamless workflow and data security. 

Currently available for **Android** (with Windows support coming soon), this app provides premium features completely free of cost.

## ✨ Key Features
*   **📦 Advanced Inventory Management**: Easily track stock levels, medicine batches, and expiry dates to prevent wastage and ensure customer safety.
*   **🧾 Smart Billing & PDF Generation**: Quickly generate professional bills for sales and export them directly as PDF files.
*   **📎 Supplier Bill Attachment**: Keep your accounts transparent and organized by attaching digital copies/photos of physical bills from your suppliers.
*   **♻️ Data Recovery (Trash/Restore)**: Accidentally deleted an item? No worries! Our restore feature allows you to recover deleted inventory items easily.
*   **🔄 Import/Export Data**: Keep your data safe and portable. Easily export your database for backups or import data to get started quickly.
*   **⚡ Auto-Update**: Stay up-to-date with the latest features and bug fixes automatically without manual hassle.
*   **💻 Cross-Platform (WIP)**: Currently optimized for Android devices, with active development for a dedicated Windows version.
*   **🔓 100% Free & Open Source**: No hidden charges, no subscription fees—completely free for a lifetime.
